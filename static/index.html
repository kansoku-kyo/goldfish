Welcome to Goldfish

1. Get a deck

Open the ☰ menu and use "Import from disk…" to pick a folder of card images. Name each file after the card, with " xN" for the number of copies, e.g. "Dragon x3.png". An optional "cardback.png" is used as the card back.

No decks found yet

2. Start

Pick a deck in the ☰ menu and press New game. 7 cards are drawn to your hand.

3. Play

Drag cards from your hand to the field. Double-click a card to tap it. Right-click a card for its menu.

4. Stream

☰ → Open stream window, then share that window. Your hand and deck contents stay hidden there; deck and trash viewers are mirrored.

Switch language any time from ☰ → Language / 言語.
